===== SECOND ATS CALL (in /api/calculate-ats) =====
Location: src/app/api/calculate-ats/route.ts

Prompt analyzes SAME resume + keywords added (tailored).
Uses Groq to estimate ATS match.
Score range: 0-100

THE PROBLEM:
- Both use temperature=0 (deterministic)
- BUT different prompts = different scoring scales
- Groq might score original resume conservatively (36%)
- Groq might score tailored resume optimistically (85%)
- The "improvement" is just Groq being inconsistent

ANALOGY:
- Ask Person A: "Rate this resume 0-100"
- Person A says: "36/100 - missing keywords"
- Ask Person B: "Rate this tailored resume 0-100"
- Person B says: "85/100 - has keywords now!"
- Same document, different evaluators = big difference
